Many data engineers spend significant time managing compatibility and getting best performance across multiple analytics engines. To help solve this pain point, we are excited to announce gcs-analytics-core , a new open-source Java library designed to centralize and accelerate analytics optimizations for Google Cloud Storage (GCS) . With this, you get the flexibility to select your preferred analytics engine while achieving high performance on GCS.
