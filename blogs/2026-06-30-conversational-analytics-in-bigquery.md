Google announced general availability of Conversational Analytics in BigQuery, letting teams query data and generate reports using natural language powered by Gemini models. The feature adds visible agentic reasoning, context citations, and proactive disambiguation, with MoneySuperMarket reporting analysis that used to take weeks now takes minutes.
